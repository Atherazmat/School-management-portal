👩‍🏫 Faculty Attendance & Payroll

Each late arrival deducts ¼ of a day's pay right away, so 4 late arrivals in a calendar month add up to exactly 1 day's pay deducted. Explicit absences deduct a full day's pay.

Teachers can also be marked present/late/absent from the ✅ Mark Attendance kiosk by entering their roll number, just like students.

🧾 Student Fee Records

Track fees due and collected per student. Recurring fees automatically re-open as unpaid on the 1st of every month. Late fine policy: paid by the 10th — no fine; paid from the 11th–20th — a one-time fine; paid from the 21st onward — a further one-time fine on top. These are flat one-time penalties, not a daily charge.
